FORMAT: Tab-separated text file, no header

================================================================================
NOTES ON DATA INTERPRETATION
================================================================================

- All forces/velocities are NON-DIMENSIONAL
  To dimensionalize: multiply by appropriate reference values
  (Reference length L, velocity U, density ρ, etc.)

- Simulation uses PERIODIC boundary conditions
  Body can move through domain boundaries

- NEGATIVE X-VELOCITY = Swimming to the LEFT (standard convention)
  NEGATIVE DRAG = Resistance force
  POSITIVE DRAG = Thrust force

- Time resolution varies:
  Body kinematics: every timestep (~0.0001 s)
  Visualization: every 40 timesteps (~0.004 s)

- Duplicate columns (e.g., columns 5-7 in many files) may represent:
  * Alternative calculation methods
  * Numerical artifacts
  * Debugging outputs
  Generally use columns 2-4 as primary data
